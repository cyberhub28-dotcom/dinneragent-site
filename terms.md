You need to be 18 or older to use Dinner Agent. You’re responsible for the people you add to your household and the information you enter.

## Chef is AI
Chef’s answers, plans, recipes, prices and voice, and what the app reads from receipts and recipes, come from AI (Claude by Anthropic, and Grok by xAI on calls and read-aloud) and can be wrong. Check amounts, times and temperatures, and check scanned items and imported recipes before you save them. Prices are typical estimates and your store may differ. Built-in recipe photos are AI-generated images, not photos of real dishes. On a call, Chef can hear other people in the room, so let them know before you start.

## Allergies and food safety
The app checks recipes against the allergies and diets you enter, by ingredient name, but it can’t guarantee a food is safe. Ingredients and labels change, and imported or AI-written recipes can be wrong. Always read labels. Food safety guidance in the app comes from USDA.

- Unused minutes don’t carry over.

Free week: new subscribers may get a free week from Apple, once per Apple Account for each subscription group. During the free week, Chef answers in chat with shorter answers; calls with Chef start when your plan does. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you won’t be charged. Otherwise your plan starts and Apple charges your Apple Account.

Fair monthly limit: each household’s AI use (Chef, Help, scans, recipe imports, read-aloud and calls) has a fair monthly limit based on its plan. Very heavy use first switches Chef to shorter answers. If the limit is reached, AI pauses until the 1st of the next month, and built-in answers and everything else keep working. Call minutes come out of the same limit.

Renewal: subscriptions renew automatically at the same price and length until you cancel. Your Apple Account is charged within 24 hours before each period ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or in the app under Settings → Manage subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. If our AI costs spike unexpectedly, Chef may pause for everyone until it’s fixed, and built-in features keep working. Refunds come from Apple (see Refund policy).
