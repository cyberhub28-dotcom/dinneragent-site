---
title: Dinner Agent Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy
---

Effective October 15, 2026. Last updated October 17, 2026.

This page is for people in Washington, Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws. It adds to our Privacy Policy. Dinner Agent is made by Zack Gabbard ("we", "us"). Contact: support@cyberhubsoftware.com.

## What we collect
Allergies, food intolerances and diets you enter for people in your household, for example "peanut allergy" or "vegetarian". We don't collect any other health data.

## Why
Only to check meals, recipes, swaps and Chef's suggestions against them, and so Chef can answer you safely. We never use them for ads, and we never use them to infer anything else about you.

## Where it comes from
Only from you: when you add or edit a profile, or when you type or say it to Chef.

## Where it's kept
On your iPhone. If you turn on Share your kitchen, it's also kept in your iCloud account and shared, through Apple, with the people you invite. Our server doesn't store it, except a Chef answer you choose to report (kept up to 1 year).

## Who receives it
Only when Chef is on: Anthropic (Claude), and xAI (Grok) on calls and steps read aloud, so Chef can answer you. Names from profiles are swapped for labels like "Person A" first. Neither company uses it to train AI. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone else.

## Your rights

- See it: Settings → Household shows every profile.
- Delete it: edit or delete a profile, or Settings → Delete account.
- Stop sharing it: turn Chef off in Settings → Chef.
- Ask us anything, or appeal a decision: email support@cyberhubsoftware.com. We reply within 45 days.
