---
title: Dinner Agent Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy
---

Effective October 15, 2026. Last updated November 16, 2026.

This page is for people in Washington, Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws. It adds to our Privacy Policy. Dinner Agent is made by CyberHub Software, LLC ("we", "us"). Contact: support@cyberhubsoftware.com.

## What we collect
Allergies, food intolerances and diets you enter for people in your household, for example "peanut allergy" or "vegetarian". We don't collect any other health data. If you choose to tell Chef about a health condition, it is sent to Anthropic, or to xAI on a call, to answer you, like anything else you type or say. Chef is told not to save it.

## Why
Only to check meals, recipes, swaps and Chef's suggestions against them, and so Chef can use your household's allergy list when answering. We never use them for ads, and we never use them to infer anything else about you. We don't use your information to train AI models, and our AI partners don't either.

## Where it comes from
Only from you: when you add or edit a profile, or when you type or say it to Chef.

Before the first allergy or diet is saved on your iPhone, the app asks for your OK and links this page. If you say no, the app saves no new allergies or diets on that iPhone, and it can't check meals for them. When you add someone else's, you confirm they agreed, or that you're their parent or guardian.

## Where it's kept
On your iPhone. If you turn on Share your kitchen, it's also kept in your iCloud account and shared, through Apple, with the people you invite. Our server doesn't store it, except a Chef answer you choose to report (kept up to 1 year), or the kitchen notes Chef uses during a call, which are deleted when the call ends. Before a report is sent, names, allergies and diets from your profiles are swapped for labels like "Person A", "[allergy]" and "[diet]".

## Who receives it
Only when Chef is on: Anthropic (Claude), and xAI (Grok) on calls and steps read aloud, so Chef can answer you. Names from profiles are swapped for labels like "Person A" first. Neither company uses it to train AI. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone else. Cloudflare, our server host, carries Chef's requests between your iPhone and these companies and doesn't keep what's in them.

Each person in a shared kitchen decides for their own profile. If someone says no, no phone in the kitchen sends their allergies or diet to Anthropic or xAI.

## Your rights

- See it: Settings → Household shows every profile.
- Delete it: edit or delete a profile, or Settings → Delete account. In a shared kitchen, Delete account removes your own profile; the others' profiles stay with them. When we delete it, we also tell our partners who handled it, like Anthropic and xAI for Chef, to delete it.
- Take back your OK: You can take back your OK at any time in Settings → Allergies and diets. When you do, Dinner Agent stops using your allergies and diets right away, erases your profile and any children's profiles you added, including from your shared kitchen, and stops sending them to Chef. Other adults keep their own profiles. Meals won't be checked for those allergies until you give your OK again.
- Stop sharing it: turn Chef off in Settings → Chef.
- Ask us anything, or appeal a decision: email support@cyberhubsoftware.com. We reply within 45 days. If we decline your request, reply to our answer to appeal. We'll answer your appeal within 60 days. If we deny your appeal, we'll explain why and tell you how to contact your state Attorney General to file a complaint.
